Our Heritage

The Living Legend
of Amer

"Built in the court of the Kachwaha dynasty, every stone wall whispers centuries of royal stories."

Nestled in the shadow of the magnificent Amer Fort, our haveli has stood witness to Maharajas, poets, and wanderers for over a century and a half. Each hand-carved jharokha, each latticed corridor, each sun-drenched courtyard carries the soul of Rajputana — restored with reverence, offered with warmth.

Culinary Heritage

Flavours of the
Royal Kitchen

Our kitchens carry the legacy of Rajput royal banquets. From slow-cooked laal maas to saffron-kissed phirni, every dish is a ceremony. Dine under open skies in our haveli courtyard or in the intimate lamp-lit zenana hall.

Sheesh Mahal Dining Hall

Evening dinners beneath glittering mirror-work ceilings — traditional Rajasthani thalis with live folk music.

The Courtyard

Al fresco breakfast and high tea beside the central fountain, surrounded by marigolds and jasmine.

Amer Bar & Lounge

Sundowner cocktails and artisan chai, served with sweeping views of the illuminated fort at dusk.
